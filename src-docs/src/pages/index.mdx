# Introduction

Recur is a desktop app for macOS and Linux where coding agents don't stop until the work is right. Give a task its own worktree, pair with an agent, or hand it to a Loop: a team of agents that plans, builds, and reviews against a bar you set.

Each chat picks its own model and one of five modes: **Ask**, **Plan**, **Agent**, **Review**, or **Loop**. Your workspace stays on your machine; your chosen model provider receives the prompts and code context it needs. GitHub is optional for local work.

## What sets Recur apart

- **Done means reviewed.** A read-only reviewer files findings, and the work goes round until it clears your bar. See [Loop mode](/concepts/loop).
- **Nothing is final.** Every turn is a checkpoint you can inspect, rewind, or fork. See [Review & checkpoints](/concepts/review).
- **Your process, your rules.** Loops, roles, and policies live in bundles you can read and rewrite. See [Bundles](/bundles).
- **Works while you sleep.** Prompts and whole loops run on their own clock. See [Schedules](/concepts/schedules).

## Concepts

- [Workspaces](/concepts/workspaces): isolated tasks, your working copy, and landing changes.
- [Chat context](/concepts/chat-context): files, selected code, terminals, and artifacts.
- [Chat modes](/concepts/modes): choose how much to delegate, and hand subtasks to child workspaces.
- [Loop mode](/concepts/loop): specialist agents, review cycles, policies, and PRs.
- [Review & checkpoints](/concepts/review): inspect changes and return to an earlier turn.
- [Pull requests](/concepts/pull-requests): review, comment, fix checks, and merge.
- [Issues](/concepts/issues): GitHub and Linear issues, and starting workspaces from them.
- [Inbox](/concepts/inbox): find work that needs your attention.
- [Schedules](/concepts/schedules): run saved prompts once or on a recurring schedule.
