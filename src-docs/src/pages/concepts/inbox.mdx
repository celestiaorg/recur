# Inbox

Open **Inbox** in the sidebar to see work across your projects, including scheduled runs and agent tasks.

| Group | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you** | Questions, permissions, or other work waiting for your attention |
| **Working** | Tasks with work in progress |
| **Pick up** | Work you can return to or inspect next |

Filter by project, choose a group, or enable **Unread** to narrow the list. Open a workspace to answer its question, inspect the diff, or continue chatting.

Read status tracks what you have seen. Marking an item read does not answer a pending question, resolve findings, or finish its task.

The **Ask Recur** panel lets you ask about your work from Inbox. For recurring work, create a [schedule](/concepts/schedules); each run gets its own workspace to inspect.
